- Libraries
	* [LuaJIT](https://luajit.org) by Mike Pall (MIT), via the [LeonTheo02 fork](https://github.com/LeonTheo02/LuaJIT) adapted for the game
	* [curl](https://curl.se) (curl license)
	* [zlib-ng](https://github.com/zlib-ng/zlib-ng) (zlib license)
	* [libpsl](https://github.com/rockdaboot/libpsl) (MIT) with the [Public Suffix List](https://publicsuffix.org) (MPL-2.0)
	* [Wren](https://wren.io) by Robert Nystrom (MIT)
	* [Mini-XML](https://www.msweet.org/mxml/) by Michael R Sweet (Apache-2.0), via the [segabl fork](https://github.com/segabl/mxml)
	* [SafetyHook](https://github.com/cursey/safetyhook) by cursey (BSL-1.0), inline hooking
	* [Zydis](https://github.com/zyantific/zydis) by zyantific (MIT), disassembler used by SafetyHook
	* [subhook](https://github.com/Zeex/subhook) by Zeex (BSD-2-Clause), used for hooking until the switch to SafetyHook
	* lookup8 hash by Bob Jenkins (public domain), used for idstrings

The license texts of these libraries are in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`.
